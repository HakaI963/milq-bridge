FreeMilk free models

      
FreeMilk's free catalog (GPT-6 Astra/Sol/Luna, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, GLM 5.3 Flash) is served by their API behind an Apple device attestation gate — verified in the decompile (docs/REVERSE.md). This bridge becomes the client: it imports your own FreeMilk session + catalog once, then every free model answers through it like any OpenAI API. 

      

        
        
      

      

    


    

      
1 · Sign in to FreeMilk (phone browser)

      
    
        
  1. Open account.milq.ai and sign in with Google (or Apple).
    2. 
        
  2. Keep that tab; your session cookie proves the account.
    3. 
      

      
2 · Import your session (two ways)

      
Quick paste — copy the milq session cookie value (rooted phone: any cookie viewer / browser devtools via chrome://flags) and send it below. 
Full capture (recommended) — run the Termux one-liner (below) while opening FreeMilk's pages; it auto-pushes session + catalog.

      

        
        
      

      

      

      

    


    

      
3 · Termux capture (one command)

      
In Termux on this phone — runs the proxy + auto-import; open FreeMilk-related pages (or replay app traffic if you ever mirror an iOS session) and the catalog lands here automatically.

      

      
      
The capture tool, source: capture/milq_capture.py. It only reads traffic from your own device and your own account.
